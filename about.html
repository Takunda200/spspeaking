The Man Behind The Method

Dr. Blessing Mandipira

Dr. Blessing Mandipira is not just a teacher; he is a practitioner. With a background in competitive debating and corporate training, he noticed a gap in the education system: we are taught what to think, but rarely how to speak.

He founded the School of Public Speaking to bridge this gap. His unique "Psychology-First" approach tackles the root cause of stage fright before teaching the mechanics of speech.

"When you change the way you speak, you change the way the world treats you."
